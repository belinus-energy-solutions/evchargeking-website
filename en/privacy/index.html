About us - Privacy statement

evChargeking is a Belgian company that offers charging accessories to owners of electric vehicles, with a slight focus on Tesla models.

For the Tesla Model S, Model X and Model 3 we developed a small application that is built into the connector on the vehicle side, which lets you open the charge port very easily, and also stop charging and unlock the connector at the push of a button, without having to use the car key or the touchscreen. A very handy solution that all our customers greatly appreciate!

We sell our products only online and through distributors.

The aim of EVChargeking is to keep providing owners of electric vehicles with the most recent and practical solutions for charging their vehicles. We are convinced that the future of driving is electric!
